Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BZlikely (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit the BZlikely website or contact us. BZlikely is currently an early-stage website project. Publisher accounts, affiliate tracking and payment services are not currently active through this website.

Important: The publisher-interest form currently opens an email draft in your own email application. The form does not submit information to a BZlikely database or store it on this website. Your information reaches us only if you choose to send that email.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us by email or send a publisher-interest enquiry, we may receive information you include, such as your name, email address, country, promotion platform and the contents of your message. Please do not send passwords, bank details, identity-document numbers or other sensitive information by ordinary email.

2. How we use information

We may use information you send us to respond to your enquiry, communicate about possible brand or publisher opportunities, maintain business correspondence, and protect the website and our services from misuse.

3. Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit a hosted website, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, device, requested page and access time for delivery, security and operational purposes. GitHub handles this information under its own policies. We do not operate our own website database or analytics system on this static website at present.

For information about GitHub's practices, review GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. Email and third-party services

If you choose to send an enquiry, your email is handled by the email provider and services you use, as well as the recipient email provider. Their own privacy terms apply. This website may also link to third-party websites. We do not control those websites or their privacy practices.

5. Sharing and selling information

We do not sell personal information submitted to us. We do not share it with brands or publishers for marketing by them. We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to respond to a lawful request, protect rights or safety, or address fraud, security or legal issues.

6. Retention and security

We keep enquiry emails for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the relationship or maintain appropriate business records, and then delete or archive them when no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations. No method of email transmission or internet storage is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information.

7. International visitors

BZlikely is intended to communicate with people in different countries. If you contact us, your information may be processed by email or hosting providers in countries other than your own, according to those providers' systems and terms.

8. Children

This website is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information you have sent us, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask us to delete an enquiry where we are able to do so. We may need to retain some information for legitimate business, security or legal reasons.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website and business develop. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. If we later introduce accounts, tracking, analytics or payment processing, this policy will need to be updated before those features are launched.

This page is a practical starting policy for the website's current limited features, not a guarantee of compliance with every country's laws and not legal advice.
